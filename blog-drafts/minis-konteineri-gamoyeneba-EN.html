One of the most common questions before buying a glass container is whether you can use it in the microwave, the oven, and the freezer. The short answer is yes, in all three, as long as you follow a few simple rules. Below we cover each case and the one safety principle that matters most.

In the microwave

The body of a glass container is safe for the microwave. The key rule is to remove or slightly open the lid. Plastic or bamboo lids are not made for the microwave, and a tightly sealed, airtight container builds up pressure inside as it heats. So always reheat with the lid off or loosened.

In the oven

This depends on the type of glass. Borosilicate, or heat resistant glass, is suitable for the oven. Always check the temperature limit stated for your specific product. Never put the lid in the oven. Also, do not place a cold container straight from the fridge into a hot oven, because a sudden temperature change can crack the glass.

In the freezer

Yes, a glass container stores well in the freezer. Follow two rules:

  • Do not fill it to the top. Food expands as it freezes, and a full container can crack. Leave a little free space at the top.
  • Avoid sudden temperature changes. Thaw a frozen container in the fridge or at room temperature first, and only then reheat it.

In the dishwasher

The glass body washes safely in the dishwasher. A bamboo lid, however, should be washed by hand and then dried, so it does not crack or warp.

The one key rule: sudden temperature change

The main reason glass cracks is thermal shock, that is a sudden move from cold to hot. Do not transfer a container from the freezer straight into a hot oven, and do not pour boiling water into frozen glass. A gradual change is always safe.
